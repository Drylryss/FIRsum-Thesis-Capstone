# Research status — 27 October 2026

## Deployed design

LegalBERT labels source sentences. Evidence selection uses those labels and structural attribution checks to choose FACT, ISSUE and RULING evidence. Frozen `nsi319/legal-pegasus` generates raw summaries. The final presentation retains source excerpts when literal-source verification rejects generated wording. Raw model predictions remain inspectable separately from final drafts.

The active classifier is `models/legalbert_balanced_v2`, selected through `model_config.json`. Neither the recent classifier candidates nor a LegalPegasus fine-tuning adapter was deployed. Source-grounding version is v13 and presentation version is v14. New generation audit fields are available in code after restarting a running server.

## Included improvements

- PDF text preparation and citation-boundary handling.
- Separate raw classifier, raw generator and final-summary workspace stages.
- Evidence attribution, quotation boundaries and preservation of the deciding Court's disposition.
- Contract-term, complaint-context and adopted statutory-reasoning coverage improvements.
- Source-backed delivery, artifact flags and conservative issue handling.
- Training-only annotation revision scripts with explicit AI provenance and protected case partitions.
- Generator diagnostics for token budgets, decoding settings and reduction stages.

## Classifier experiments

The deployed classifier's provisional validation result is 100/112 correct (89.29%), macro-F1 0.5798. Validation has only three ISSUE sentences and one OTHER sentence, so these category estimates are weak and are not legal-summary accuracy.

An annotation candidate reached 101/112 (90.18%), macro-F1 0.5947, but worsened material summary coverage in one development case and was not promoted.

A subsequent consistency revision added 28 exact source spans from existing training cases and withheld one ambiguous ISSUE example. Its 441 training records comprise FACT 196, ISSUE 18, RULING 208 and OTHER 19. The candidate scored 100/112, macro-F1 0.5566; ISSUE recall fell from 3/3 to 2/3. It was not promoted. All 112 validation and 58 test records were preserved; no new final-test evaluation was performed. AI annotation is not attorney approval.

## Frozen LegalPegasus experiments

On Mayor and Adelfa development cases, all six selected baseline inputs fit within the 1,024-token limit. Shorter decoding reproduced all six baseline texts exactly. Moving dispositions first still produced false parties, courts and outcomes. Adding source captions and Philippine Supreme Court identity corrected court naming but left material factual errors. No experimental setting was made the production default.

Ten distinct new role generations were compared with saved baseline outputs. All were rejected by the literal-source gate. That rejection rate is not a factual-accuracy score: even a correct abstractive paraphrase fails an exact-wording gate. Specific hallucinations were identified through AI source inspection, not independent attorney scoring.
